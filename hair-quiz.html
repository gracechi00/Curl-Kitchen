Step 1 of 5 — Hair Type

What's your curl pattern?

Select the option that best describes your natural texture.

➖ Straight (1A–1C) Little to no curl, tends to be silky or fine, can get oily
〰️ Wavy (2A–2C) Loose S-shaped waves, some frizz, tends to fall flat
🌀 Curly (3A–3C) Defined spirals and ringlets, can range from loose to tight
🌿 Coily (4A–4B) Tight coils with lots of shrinkage, dense and full
✨ Kinky (4C) Very tight zig-zag pattern, significant shrinkage, very versatile
🌿 I have locs Currently wearing locs — retwist or loc care services
