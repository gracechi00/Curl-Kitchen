Our Story

More than a salon.
A community.

Curl Kitchen Salon is the premier salon for naturally curly haircare services and beauty needs. We've been in business since the Autumn of 2013, when we started as a natural hair care and beauty boutique on Chicago's South Side.

We are dedicated to serving our community by providing the best quality hair services to the Chicagoland area. We are an appointment-only salon and we pride ourselves on never double-booking.

We are for naturally curly women, operated by naturally curly women, and owned by a naturally curly woman.
